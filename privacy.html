Privacy

We count visits so we can make the site better. We do it without cookies and without tracking you.

What we measure

Which pages are viewed, how long a page is on screen, how far down it is scrolled, which projects, tour videos and buttons get attention, whether the contact form is used, and how fast the site loads. We also note the general area (country and city), the kind of device and browser, and which website or link brought you here.

What we do not do

  • No cookies and no advertising trackers. Nothing is shared with Google, Facebook or any ad network.
  • Your IP address is never stored. To count unique visitors, a one-way code is made from your connection details and today's date. It changes every day and cannot be traced back to you.
  • Names, email addresses and what you type in the chat or the contact form are never part of the statistics.
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we count nothing at all.

How long we keep it

Statistics are deleted automatically after 13 months.

Contact form and chat

If you send a message or ask to talk to the team in the chat, your message and email address go to us so we can reply. If you record a voice note, the recording goes to us the same way and is only used to listen and reply. Questions the chat could not answer are saved without any personal details so we can teach it better answers.
